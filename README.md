Simple mod for clearing the after effects of Archaon End Times crisis.

To use, drop the .pack files into <game>/data folder.
Because the original logic is bugged on several levels, this just goes through the list of chaos_wasteland condition and clears it. It's a crude approach but it works.

I recommend enabling it, loading your save to clear the leftover climate, save your game, and then disable the mod to reduce the chance of it interfering with other effects.
